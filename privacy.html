```html BAPUL Account Deletion
BAPUL Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your BAPUL account and associated data, please send an email to:

support@bapul.app

Required Information

  • Registered email address
  • Account deletion request

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account information
  • Saved recipes
  • Shopping lists
  • User-submitted recipes
  • Profile data

Retention Period

All account data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of verification.

For questions, contact: support@bapul.app

```
