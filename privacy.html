BAPUL Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 7, 2026

BAPUL ("BAPUL," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the BAPUL mobile application and related services.

BAPUL is a recipe recommendation app that helps users discover recipes, match recipes with refrigerator ingredients, manage shopping lists, save recipes, and personalize recommendations based on taste and profile preferences.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide and improve the BAPUL service.

Account Information

  • Email address

App Usage and User Content

  • Saved recipes
  • Shopping list items
  • Ingredient selections
  • Refrigerator or pantry ingredient selections entered by the user
  • Taste, cooking, and profile preferences
  • Basic app interaction data needed to operate and improve the app

2. Information We Do Not Collect

BAPUL does not collect or process the following types of information:

  • Payment information
  • Precise location data
  • Health data
  • Government identification numbers
  • Biometric data
  • Personal data for sale to third parties

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To recommend recipes based on ingredients and preferences
  • To save and sync recipes across the app
  • To create and manage shopping lists
  • To improve recipe discovery and ingredient matching
  • To personalize the user experience
  • To maintain app security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues
  • To communicate with users about support requests, account issues, or service updates

4. Third-Party Services

BAPUL uses trusted third-party services to operate the app. These services may process limited information as necessary to provide their functionality.

Supabase

We use Supabase for backend services, including authentication, database storage, and secure data access.

Expo

We use Expo to build, test, and operate the mobile app experience. Expo services may help support app updates, development, and diagnostics.

Google Play Services

For Android users, Google Play Services and Google Play may process information according to Google's own privacy practices, including app distribution, device compatibility, crash reporting, security, and related platform services.

These third-party services process data according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information using appropriate safeguards for a beta-stage consumer app.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may share limited information only in the following situations:

  • With service providers that help us operate BAPUL
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of BAPUL, our users, or others
  • In connection with a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, if applicable in the future

7. Account Deletion and Data Deletion Requests

You may request account deletion or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at:

  • privacy@bapul.app
  • support@bapul.app

Please include the email address associated with your BAPUL account so we can identify and process your request.

After receiving a deletion request, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify your account information and associated app data, unless we are required to retain certain information for legal, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

BAPUL is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to BAPUL, please contact us at privacy@bapul.app.

9. International Users

BAPUL may be operated from the United States and may use service providers located in the United States or other countries. By using BAPUL, you understand that your information may be processed in countries where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as BAPUL changes or as legal, operational, or platform requirements evolve.

When we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the Effective Date above. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or other appropriate channels.
